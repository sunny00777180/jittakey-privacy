Privacy Policy

JittaKey – Japanese Input Keyboard

Applies to: iOS and Android
Last updated: November 19, 2026

1. Personal Information Collected

This application does not collect or transmit any personal information, such as what you type, contacts, or location data.
To improve conversion accuracy, frequently used conversion history (learning data) and app settings (e.g., alphabet display and small kana display ON/OFF) are stored only on your device.

2. About Full Access Permission (iOS only)

This application requests "Full Access" for haptic feedback (vibration), the learning feature (saving learning data), and applying dictionary updates downloaded by the app to the keyboard.
Even with Full Access enabled, no input content or personal information is collected or transmitted outside your device.

3. Network Usage

This application connects to the internet only to download dictionary files from a public repository (GitHub) to keep dictionary data up to date. During this process, no input content or personally identifiable information is sent.

4. Third-Party Sharing and External Transmission

Because this application does not collect personal information, it never provides such information to third parties or transmits it to external servers.

5. Data Retention & Deletion

This application does not store personal information on any server. When the application is deleted, all learning data and settings stored on the device are removed.
You can reset the learning data at any time in the app settings or from the menu in the keyboard.

6. User Rights

You can remove information stored on your device at any time by resetting the learning data in the app settings or from the menu in the keyboard, or by deleting the application.

7. Children's Privacy

This application is not directed at any particular age group. We do not collect personal information from any user, regardless of age.
